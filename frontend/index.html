Refresh Rate — 60 Hz vs 165 Hz

For each measure: every participant's value in the 60 Hz and 165 Hz conditions, and the difference (165 Hz − 60 Hz). Per-participant values are the median of that condition's included trials (robust to skew/artifact); the group headline is the mean Δ of those per-participant differences (matching the paired t-test), with median Δ / SD / 95% CI alongside. Conditions are assigned from each participant's refresh-rate ordering, not block number. Click any chart to see how a number was computed.
