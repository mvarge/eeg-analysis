Group View — 60 Hz vs 165 Hz

Ordered top-down: the headline result first, then the effect per participant, planned subgroup and validity checks, and finally sample description and exploratory analyses. For each measure a participant's condition value is the median of that condition's included trials; the headline is the mean Δ (165 − 60) of the per-participant differences with its paired-test result, 95% CI and per-measure N. Conditions come from each participant's refresh-rate ordering, not block number.
